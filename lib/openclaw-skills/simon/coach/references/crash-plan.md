# Crash plan, written while well

Private `coach/crash-plan.md`, not the day plan or a crisis plan. On a crash,
read and follow the current plan with Simon. Do not replace it with ambitious
activation, a new backlog, a lesson or a cause/diagnosis. Safety overrides it.

In the **Sunday look-back**, after the existing project review and the patterns
note changes, read the exact path. If absent, offer to draft the first plan
**tonight (Sunday 2026-11-15)**, while well enough; if postponed, leave it open
for the next requested/weekly review without repeated daily offers. Existing
plan means review only if useful, not redraft every week. If too depleted,
use immediate demand reduction and defer planning; no required homework.

Use the existing wind-down **cards and options** style: current section,
2–4 concrete choices plus plain chat/custom/skip, one question at a time.
Read `.wind-down-references/cards.md` for existing presentation/tool rules.
No new visual design, long questionnaire or invented defaults. After a successful
show_widget, don't duplicate its options in prose or say you haven't seen it
display: lack of visual access is not evidence of failure. At most two short
sentences outside the card, including “You can also type your answer here.”
Use full text/options fallback only on a tool failure or his request. No TEST
bookkeeping panels; operator read-back checks those files separately.
Examples are choices, never saved answers. Draft these five sections in Simon's words:

1. **Trigger signs:** his observable signs that mean start the plan; distinguish
   his reports from evidence-backed general signs. No fixed diagnostic threshold.
2. **Projects that pause:** select from the same interim projects list; reference
   stable IDs/names, not a duplicate status authority. Drafting is not parking.
3. **Drop or postpone:** what can go, and what can wait; no silent deletion of
   tasks/calendar commitments or messages to other people.
4. **Minimum day:** food, sleep, school run. Ask what is truly needed and feasible;
   this is the plan's minimum, not body-upkeep coaching or nutritional advice.
   Keep options to those three essentials, not walks, exercise or new health goals.
5. **Gentle restart:** one small restart and the signs he's ready; no catch-up
   sprint or debt for resting. He decides when to resume paused projects.

Show the five-section draft for his correction/confirmation before writing.
Save agreed content only, read-back before “saved”; incomplete fields stay
explicitly pending. A failed save never holds the talk open. End with one next
step, not five assignments. No project/service/calendar mutation just from
saving the plan; any later agreed park/resume uses the existing list's verified
write path. No recording this plan in USER.md, MEMORY.md or a shared summary.

For an explicitly fake dry run use **coach/TEST-crash-plan.md** only, label the
draft TEST, and never treat it as a real crash, park real projects, write real
daily notes or start quiet policy. Operator removes it with read-back.
